# Customer Journey Map

## Aware
- **Trigger Event**: Creator or indie hacker realizes the need to find profitable software product ideas.
- **Friction Points**: Lack of awareness about tools that can help in product discovery, overwhelming amount of information, difficulty in validating ideas.
- **User Emotions**: Frustration, confusion, overwhelm.
- **Opportunities to Delight**: Provide educational content, case studies, and success stories to guide users.
- **Metric**: Number of unique visitors to the product website or landing page.

## Consider
- **Trigger Event**: User becomes aware of Product Scout and starts evaluating it against other tools.
- **Friction Points**: Comparison with other tools, understanding the unique value proposition, pricing concerns.
- **User Emotions**: Curiosity, skepticism, hope.
- **Opportunities to Delight**: Offer free trials, detailed feature comparisons, testimonials from satisfied users.
- **Metric**: Number of users who sign up for a free trial or request a demo.

## Try
- **Trigger Event**: User signs up for a free trial or starts using the basic version of Product Scout.
- **Friction Points**: Learning curve, integration with existing tools, initial setup.
- **User Emotions**: Excitement, anxiety, impatience.
- **Opportunities to Delight**: Provide onboarding tutorials, live chat support, quick start guides.
- **Metric**: Number of active users during the trial period, time spent on the platform.

## Adopt
- **Trigger Event**: User decides to purchase a subscription or upgrade to a paid plan.
- **Friction Points**: Pricing, commitment, perceived value.
- **User Emotions**: Confidence, relief, satisfaction.
- **Opportunities to Delight**: Offer personalized onboarding, exclusive content, regular updates.
- **Metric**: Conversion rate from trial to paid subscription, customer lifetime value (CLV).

## Expand
- **Trigger Event**: User becomes a power user and starts exploring advanced features or additional services.
- **Friction Points**: Understanding advanced features, scaling usage, additional costs.
- **User Emotions**: Empowerment, enthusiasm, loyalty.
- **Opportunities to Delight**: Provide advanced training, exclusive webinars, loyalty programs.
- **Metric**: Number of advanced features used, revenue from upsells, net promoter score (NPS).
