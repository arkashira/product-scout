## 6. Success Metrics (90‑Day Targets)  

| Metric | Target (Day +90) | Rationale |
|--------|------------------|-----------|
| **Daily Active Users (DAU)** | **≥ 800** | ≈ 6.7 % of total sign‑ups; indicates product stickiness for idea generation. |
| **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)** | **$22,500** | 1,200 Pro users @ $19/mo → validates pricing & conversion funnel. |
| **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)** | **≤ $25** | Meets profitability threshold given LTV ≈ $600 (30 mo avg). |
| **Churn (30‑day)** | **≤ 5 %** | Early‑stage SaaS benchmark; ensures community value. |
| **Referral Rate** | **≥ 20 %** of new sign‑ups | Powered by Discord affiliate program. |
| **Content Engagement** | **≥ 3,000** total blog reads + **500** webinar attendees | Drives inbound pipeline. |

*If any metric falls > 15 % short of target, trigger a rapid‑iteration sprint (2‑week) focusing on the under‑performing channel or funnel step.*
