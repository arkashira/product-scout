# partner-targets.md

## Partner Integration Roadmap for Product-Scout

### Target Integrations

| Partner/API                | Free-Tier Limits                     | Integration Effort | Value-Add                                   | Rationale                                                                                     |
|---------------------------|-------------------------------------|--------------------|---------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Stripe**                | $0 for up to $1 million in revenue | M                  | Payment processing for product sales       | Enables creators to monetize their software products directly through the platform.           |
| **Zapier**                | 100 tasks/month                     | S                  | Automate workflows for product discovery    | Allows users to connect Product-Scout with other tools they use, enhancing productivity.      |
| **Airtable**              | 1,200 records per base              | M                  | Organize product ideas and feedback         | Provides a flexible database solution for users to manage their product ideas and iterations. |
| **Notion**                | 1,000 blocks                        | S                  | Documentation and project management        | Helps creators document their product ideas and manage tasks in a collaborative environment.  |
| **Figma**                 | 3 projects                          | M                  | Design collaboration for product mockups    | Facilitates design iterations and feedback for product concepts, enhancing user experience.   |
| **Product Hunt API**      | N/A                                 | L                  | Access to trending products and feedback    | Enables users to analyze market trends and validate product ideas against successful launches. |
| **Google Analytics**      | 10 million hits/month              | M                  | Track user engagement and product performance | Provides insights into user behavior and product performance, helping refine offerings.        |
| **Slack**                 | 10 integrations                     | S                  | Team communication and updates              | Allows teams to collaborate and receive notifications about product updates and feedback.     |

### Prioritization Criteria
1. **Affiliate/Revenue-Share Potential**: Partners like Stripe and Zapier offer opportunities for revenue sharing through their affiliate programs, making them financially attractive.
2. **Integration Effort**: Focus on integrations that require a manageable effort (S/M) to ensure timely delivery and value realization.
3. **User Job Solutions**: Each integration should directly address a specific user job, enhancing the overall value proposition of Product-Scout.

### Conclusion
Integrating with these SaaS/APIs will not only enhance the functionality of Product-Scout but also provide significant value to indie hackers and creators by streamlining their product discovery and development processes.
