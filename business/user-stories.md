# User Stories for Product-Scout

## Epic 1: Idea Generation
### User Story 1
**As a** creator, **I want** to receive AI-generated product ideas based on market trends, **so that** I can quickly identify viable opportunities.

- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - The tool analyzes current market trends and user demands.
  - The generated ideas are categorized by industry and potential profitability.
  - Users can filter ideas based on their interests and expertise.
  - Ideas are presented with a brief description and potential target audience.

- **Estimated Complexity:** M

### User Story 2
**As a** creator, **I want** to input my skills and interests, **so that** the tool can tailor product ideas specifically for me.

- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Users can create a profile with their skills and interests.
  - The tool suggests ideas that align with the user's profile.
  - Users can update their profile at any time to receive new suggestions.
  - Suggestions are ranked based on relevance to the user's profile.

- **Estimated Complexity:** M

## Epic 2: Validation of Ideas
### User Story 3
**As a** creator, **I want** to validate the potential of product ideas through market data, **so that** I can ensure there is demand before investing time.

- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - The tool provides data on search volume, competition, and existing solutions.
  - Users can view historical trends for similar product ideas.
  - Validation metrics are clearly displayed for each idea.
  - Users can compare multiple ideas side-by-side.

- **Estimated Complexity:** L

### User Story 4
**As a** creator, **I want** to receive feedback from potential users on my product ideas, **so that** I can refine them before development.

- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Users can share their ideas within the platform for feedback.
  - The tool collects and displays user feedback in a structured format.
  - Users can engage with feedback providers for further clarification.
  - Feedback can be categorized by positive, negative, and neutral sentiments.

- **Estimated Complexity:** M

## Epic 3: Collaboration and Networking
### User Story 5
**As a** creator, **I want** to connect with other creators who have similar interests, **so that** we can collaborate on product development.

- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - The tool suggests potential collaborators based on shared interests and skills.
  - Users can send collaboration requests through the platform.
  - A messaging feature allows users to communicate directly.
  - Users can view profiles of potential collaborators.

- **Estimated Complexity:** M

### User Story 6
**As a** creator, **I want** to join discussions and forums about product ideas, **so that** I can gain insights and inspiration from others.

- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - The tool hosts discussion boards categorized by topics.
  - Users can post questions and share insights.
  - Popular discussions are highlighted for easy access.
  - Users can upvote helpful responses.

- **Estimated Complexity:** S

## Epic 4: Learning Resources
### User Story 7
**As a** creator, **I want** access to resources and guides on product development, **so that** I can improve my skills and knowledge.

- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - The tool provides a library of articles, videos, and tutorials.
  - Resources are categorized by skill level and topic.
  - Users can bookmark resources for future reference.
  - New resources are suggested based on user activity.

- **Estimated Complexity:** M

### User Story 8
**As a** creator, **I want** to receive personalized learning recommendations, **so that** I can focus on areas that will benefit my product development.

- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - The tool analyzes user activity and suggests relevant resources.
  - Recommendations are updated regularly based on user progress.
  - Users can provide feedback on the usefulness of resources.
  - Users can track their learning journey within the tool.

- **Estimated Complexity:** L
