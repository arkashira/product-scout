# Product Requirements Document
## Product Scout
### Overview

The Product Scout is an AI-powered product discovery tool designed to help creators identify profitable and viable software product ideas. The tool will leverage market signals, customer needs, and industry trends to provide actionable insights and recommendations for product development.

### Problem Statement

Creators and product developers often struggle to identify profitable and viable software product ideas, resulting in wasted resources, failed products, and missed opportunities. The current process of identifying product ideas relies heavily on manual research, intuition, and trial-and-error, leading to inconsistent and unreliable results.

### Target Users

* Creators: independent developers, startups, and entrepreneurs looking to build successful software products
* Product Developers: teams and organizations responsible for developing and launching software products
* Business Leaders: executives and decision-makers seeking to identify new business opportunities and revenue streams

### Goals

* Provide creators with a reliable and efficient way to identify profitable and viable software product ideas
* Reduce the risk of product failure by leveraging data-driven insights and market validation
* Increase the success rate of software products by identifying unmet customer needs and industry trends

### Key Features

#### Priority 1: Core Features

1. **Market Signal Analysis**: Integrate with external data sources to analyze market trends, customer needs, and industry developments
2. **Idea Generation**: Utilize AI algorithms to generate a list of potential product ideas based on market signal analysis
3. **Idea Validation**: Provide a validation framework to assess the viability and profitability of generated product ideas
4. **Recommendation Engine**: Offer personalized recommendations for product development based on user input, market signals, and idea validation

#### Priority 2: Secondary Features

1. **User Input and Feedback**: Allow users to provide input on their interests, skills, and preferences to inform idea generation
2. **Collaboration Tools**: Enable users to share and discuss product ideas with team members and stakeholders
3. **Integration with Axentx BRAIN**: Integrate with the Axentx BRAIN to leverage company knowledge, memory, and datasets
4. **Data Visualization**: Provide interactive dashboards and visualizations to help users understand market signals and idea validation results

#### Priority 3: Nice-to-Have Features

1. **Natural Language Processing**: Integrate NLP capabilities to analyze user feedback and market signals
2. **Predictive Modeling**: Utilize predictive modeling techniques to forecast market trends and product success
3. **Gamification**: Incorporate gamification elements to encourage user engagement and idea sharing

### Success Metrics

* Number of users actively using the Product Scout tool
* Quality and relevance of generated product ideas
* User satisfaction and engagement metrics (e.g., time spent on the platform, feedback, and ratings)
* Number of successful products launched using ideas generated by the Product Scout tool

### Scope and Out-of-Scope

#### In-Scope

* Development of the Product Scout tool, including core features and secondary features
* Integration with external data sources and Axentx BRAIN
* User testing and feedback mechanisms

#### Out-of-Scope

* Development of individual software products based on generated ideas
* Marketing and promotion of the Product Scout tool
* Ongoing maintenance and support of launched products

### Assumptions and Dependencies

* Access to relevant market data and industry trends
* Integration with Axentx BRAIN and other company systems
* User adoption and engagement with the Product Scout tool

### Timeline and Milestones

* Research and planning: 2 weeks
* Development: 12 weeks
* Testing and iteration: 4 weeks
* Launch: 2 weeks

Note: The timeline and milestones are estimates and may be adjusted based on user feedback, technical complexity, and other factors.
